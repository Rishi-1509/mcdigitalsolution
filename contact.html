FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you send a technician? ▼
For bookings before 12 noon, we offer same-day service. For emergency repairs, we aim to dispatch within 2–4 hours depending on your location.
Do you use genuine spare parts? ▼
Yes, always. We use only OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) parts sourced directly from authorised distributors. All parts come with a minimum 90-day warranty.
Which water purifier brands do you service? ▼
We service all major brands — Kent, Aquaguard, Pureit, Blue Star, LG PuriCare, A.O. Smith, Livpure, Havells, V-Guard, Tata Swach, and more.
Do you provide a service warranty? ▼
Yes. All repair and service work comes with a 30-day service warranty. Replaced parts carry a 90-day parts warranty.
Which areas do you cover? ▼
We currently serve all over Chennai and its surrounding areas. Contact us to confirm availability in your area.
